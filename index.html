About

I work on robot learning and generalization. I co-founded Waddle Labs, where I build language-model agents that control robots.

Before Waddle, I studied algebraic topology at MIT's Research Science Institute and math and physics at Harvard. I am curious about how natural intelligence arises, and I like making things with 3D printers and robots.

Waddle Labs

I started Waddle Labs with Yiding (Vincent) Song. We build agents that interpret a task, inspect camera feeds, and write control code so people can test and refine what their robots do.

I’m building Waddle Labs with Yiding Song. We’re an applied AI lab building LLMs that control robots. We think that our work over the next six months will be pivotal for shaping how people work with robots.

Previously, I studied math at Harvard, where I was a TA for Math 55. In research, I’m interested in what we can learn about the brain by observing and building machine learning models.

I’m grateful to have worked on algebraic topology with Dr. David J. Lee at RSI/MIT, predictive coding with Dr. Chenguang Li, spiking neural networks with Jorin Overwiening and Prof. Haim Sompolinsky, and robot learning with Prof. Yilun Du. I’m also thankful for Quincy and my roommates. Much of this work happened there.

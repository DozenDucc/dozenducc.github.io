Hanming Ye research & robotics

I work on learning and robots.

I’m interested in how models generalize, and what changes when they have to act in the physical world.

I studied math and physics at Harvard. Before that, I worked on algebraic topology at MIT’s Research Science Institute. That background still shapes how I approach machine learning.

These days I’m building Waddle Labs with Yiding (Vincent) Song. We work on agents that use a robot’s cameras, write control code, try a task, and change course when it does not work. I spend a lot of time thinking about where that loop breaks.

My papers so far cover grokking, diffusion policies, and algebraic topology. I also think about natural intelligence. Outside work, I like making things with 3D printers.
